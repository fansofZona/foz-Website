Volleyball is the purest possession sport that nobody prices. The six rotation
slots are a state machine: you serve-receive in one alignment, the rally
resolves, and you rotate. We charted three seasons of film to build the
transition matrix, then solved the cycle for each rotation's expected points.

The result is a league table of serve-receive alignments. The gap between the
best and worst rotation in the conference was worth about a set every three
matches — the kind of edge teams leave on the table every single week:

## What the model changes about substitutions

The boring-but-valuable finding: teams that held their best passer in the
weakest rotations — even if it meant playing your all-conference hitter two
spots from her favorite one — outscored their talent. The Markov structure
makes that visible: lost serve-receive points cascade through the cycle, not
just the rally they happened in.

Our agent recommends treating rotations 2 and 5 as the "drop the ball" states
and designing lineups so the two best receivers overlap there. The film
tracking templates and the transition matrices are published under an open
license. Yes, you can send this to your coach.
