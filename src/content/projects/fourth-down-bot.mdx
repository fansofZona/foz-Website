Every autumn we build something that makes us argue at practice. This year it
is a bot that listens to our team's broadcasts, snaps the score and field
position into a win-probability model, and posts the go-for-it verdict before
the punt team trots out.

The model is a gradient-boosted classifier fit on 18 years of play-by-play.
Win probability is the usual engine: from each fourth-down state we branch
into the distributions of punt, field-goal, and go outcomes, and compare
expected win probability. The nudges surprise people, so we ship them as
before/after values, not as commandments:

## Where the model declines to have an opinion

We deliberately cap the bot: it will not grade fourth downs when the game is
blowout-ended (WP above 0.92) or in the final 40 seconds with a twelve-point
lead. Those states are where win-probability models are least reliable and
cheerleaders are most talkative.

The whole thing runs in 900 milliseconds from whistle to poster. The repo
includes the scraper, the model weights, and the full backtest where it would
have agreed with public models 83% of the time and disagreed loudly at the
margins — which is exactly where we want Garrett.
