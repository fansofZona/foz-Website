Five years of public transfer announcements, deduplicated, normalized, and
loaded into a single graph. Nodes are schools; directed edges are athlete
transfers; weights are transfer counts. The dataset fixes a common pain: every
website reports transfers differently, so cross-program comparison has always
been guesswork.

The most cited stat from the build — net exporter balance by conference. Some
leagues are tournament-driven buying floors; others are farm systems with a
steady trickle up:

Negative is an exporting conference; it replaces its draft losses with fresh
freshmen every spring. Large positive balances cluster in leagues where
basketball money travels easiest.

The full export — edges, attributes, and the scrapers that rebuilt it — is on
GitHub with a schema that takes about an hour to understand, because we wrote
the docs after we were done.

We learned the hard way that "announced move" ≠ "granted eligibility," so the
graph carries both flags. Use the eligible-only subset unless you want to
count players who never played a minute at their new school.
